How to play

Get the orb that matches the ✕ to stop on the ✕.

  • Orbs slide until they hit something: a wall or another orb. They can't stop partway.
  • To move: hover over an orb, then click the side you want it to go.
  • To move: touch an orb and drag your finger towards the side you want it to go.
  • Use the other orbs as blockers. Moving one into place can create the stop you need.

Scoring

  • Four puzzles a day, each harder than the last. Everyone gets the same set.
  • Each puzzle has a par: the fewest moves possible. Your score is your total moves over par. +0 is perfect.
  • Undo is free, so experiment as much as you like.
  • Solve shows the answer, but that puzzle won't count toward your score.

New puzzles every day at midnight.
